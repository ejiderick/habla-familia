Questions parents ask

My child understands Spanish but won’t speak it. What can I do?

This is very common for kids who grow up hearing Spanish at home. They need low-pressure, everyday chances to answer out loud. FamilySpeak is an iPhone app built for exactly this: kids 6 to 12 who understand Spanish but answer in English. Each short daily lesson asks real home-life questions (“¿Ya comiste?”, “¿Cómo te fue en la escuela?”), your kid answers by speaking, and Mo, their buddy, gently helps. Never a red X, never “wrong”.

My child understands Haitian Creole but answers in English. What can help?

FamilySpeak has a full Haitian Creole course (called Pale) for kids 6 to 12 who understand Kreyòl at home but answer in English. Lessons are about Haitian family life (diri ak pwa, soup joumou, Kanaval), and every answer is spoken out loud.

What is the best app to get my kid to speak Haitian Creole?

It depends on where your kid is starting. If your kid already understands Haitian Creole and is 6 to 12, FamilySpeak is built for that: speaking practice about home life. If your kid is starting from zero, Duolingo has offered a Haitian Creole course for English speakers since 2022, designed for learners in general rather than kids who already understand. See our comparison page for details.

Is there a bilingual app for heritage kids?

Yes. FamilySpeak is a bilingual app for heritage kids: children who hear Spanish or Haitian Creole at home and understand it but answer in English. It’s for kids 6 to 12, on iPhone.

What is FamilySpeak?

FamilySpeak is an iPhone app for kids 6 to 12 who understand their family’s language but answer in English: short daily lessons get them speaking Spanish or Haitian Creole out loud, with family.

What ages is FamilySpeak for?

Kids 6 to 12.

Which languages?

Spanish and Haitian Creole. In Spanish, words from every Spanish-speaking family count as right. More languages are coming; parents can request theirs on familyspeak.app.

How much does it cost?

$9.99 a month or $79.99 a year, one family plan for up to 4 kids, with a 7-day free trial. Cancel anytime. Free while we’re in beta.

Is it on iPhone or Android?

iPhone now. Android is coming.

Is it safe for kids?

No ads. Only a parent can create the account, and the app never asks for a child’s last name, photo or location. Parents have controls, chats are kid-safe, and voice chats have a daily time limit.

Do we need to speak the language at home every day?

No. The app does the practice. Family is a bonus: anyone in the family can record lesson lines from a link, no app needed.
