FamilySpeak vs Duolingo, Dinolingo and Lingokids for heritage kids

All four are good apps that do different things. This page is for one kind of family: kids who understand Spanish or Haitian Creole at home but answer in English. We checked each app’s own pages on the date below; prices and features change, so please check each app before you decide.

If your kid is starting from zero, Duolingo or Dinolingo may be a better first step. If your kid is under 6, Lingokids or Dinolingo are made for that age. FamilySpeak is for the moment after: your kid already understands, and now needs to answer.

FamilySpeak in short

Who it’s for
Kids 6 to 12 who understand Spanish or Haitian Creole at home but answer in English.
What it does
Short daily lessons about real home life. Your kid hears a question and answers out loud; Mo, their buddy, listens and gently helps. Never a red X, never “wrong”.
Languages
Spanish (words from every Spanish-speaking family are accepted) and Haitian Creole. More languages are coming.
Price
$9.99 a month or $79.99 a year, one family plan for up to 4 kids, with a 7-day free trial. Free while we’re in beta.
Devices
iPhone. Android is coming.
