Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

FamilySpeak is a speaking app for kids 6 to 12. It helps kids speak their family’s language. Courses today: Habla (Spanish) and Pale (Haitian Creole), with more coming. Because kids under 13 use it, we follow the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). This page explains, in plain words, what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and how you delete it.

The short version

  • Only a parent or legal guardian can create an account, and they must give permission before a child uses the app.
  • About each child we keep only a nickname, age, boy/girl, family language, and their progress. Never last names, photos, location, or contact info.
  • Your child’s voice is sent to our server to check each answer, then deleted right away, unless you turn on “Save recordings.”
  • To check answers, the recording and the question are sent to OpenAI.
  • Live voice chats with Mo and the grandparent send your child’s voice to OpenAI while the chat is open. We don’t record or save the audio or the words. We keep only the minutes, plus a few favorites Mo remembers (like a favorite food or a pet’s name), which you can see and erase.
  • No ads. We don’t sell or share data for advertising. No tracking tools, and no chatting with strangers.
  • You can delete a child’s data or your whole account anytime in the app.

1. Parent permission (COPPA)

Kids never sign up or log in on their own. A parent creates the account with an email address and password. Before any child profile can be used, the parent confirms they are 18 or older and the child’s parent or legal guardian, and gives permission for the child to use FamilySpeak. We save that permission with the date and time and the version of this notice it covered.

A parent can review what we have about their child in the parent area, ask us to delete it, or refuse further collection by deleting the child’s profile. Email hello@familyspeak.app for anything you can’t do in the app.

2. What we collect

About the parent

  • Email address and password to log in. Passwords are stored by our login provider in a protected (hashed) form; we never see them.
  • Permission record: that you agreed, when, and which notice version.
  • Settings: parent-screen language, time zone (so the weekly email arrives on Sunday), reminder time, weekly email on/off, English hints on/off, save recordings on/off.
  • Plan: during the test there are no payments. We store the plan you picked and your free-trial dates, and count paywall views and taps to see if the screen is clear.

About each child (entered by the parent)

  • Nickname (first name or nickname only; the app blocks last names), age (6 to 12), boy or girl (used for Spanish grammar such as lleno / llena), family language (course), Spanish style for Spanish learners, the buddy’s color and name (the child can keep the name Mo or choose another), and starting level.

Created while the child uses the app

  • Progress: lessons finished, stars, coins, streaks, badges, prizes, level-check results, questions to practice again, and time spent.
  • Answers: for each answer, whether it was right, the stars, and how long it took. We also keep a text transcript of what the speech checker heard (or what the child typed), with the question and result. Our small team may read these transcripts, never audio, to make speech checking fairer for kids’ voices. They are deleted when you delete the child or your account.
  • Talking with the AI grandparent (the last lesson): the conversation is checked the same way. The saved transcript has numbers removed, and the AI grandparent is built never to ask for an address, school, last name, phone, or other private details.

Voice recordings

  • The microphone is used only while your child holds the mic button.
  • Each recording goes to our server, is turned into text and checked, then deleted right after checking.
  • If you turn on Save recordings in the parent area, recordings are kept in private storage so you can listen later. Turn it off anytime; delete them by deleting the child’s data.

Live voice chats (Talk to Mo, Talk with the grandparent)

  • Your child can talk with Mo for up to 5 minutes a day, about a new everyday topic each day, and with the grandparent character for 3 minutes at the end of each unit (up to two chats a day). You can turn live chats off in the parent area, under Settings → Live voice chats.
  • During a chat the microphone stays on until the chat ends: when time runs out, when your child taps Hang up, or when they leave the screen or the app goes to the background.
  • The phone connects straight to OpenAI over an encrypted connection, using a key from our server that expires after two minutes. Our own OpenAI key never leaves our server.
  • OpenAI hears your child’s voice live, turns it into text and answers with a voice. To set up each chat we send OpenAI your child’s nickname, age, boy/girl, family language (and Spanish style), phrases from lessons they finished, and for Mo, today’s topic and the favorites Mo remembers. We also send an anonymous code made from your account, never your email, so OpenAI can spot misuse.
  • We don’t record or save the audio or the words of a live chat. The words on screen are shown only during the chat. We save the day, the child, which chat and the minutes used, to keep the daily limit and show you the minutes.
  • Mo and the grandparent are built to stay on kid-safe topics, never to ask for or repeat a last name, address, school, phone, email or photos, and to tell a child who seems upset or unsafe to talk to a grown-up they trust.

What Mo remembers

  • So chats feel friendly, Mo can remember a few small things your child shares: favorite food, color, animal, game, sport, holiday or school subject, the kind of pet they have, and the pet’s name. Nothing else.
  • Each one is a few words (30 characters at most), one of each kind. Our server refuses anything with numbers, links or emails, or with words for streets, schools or grown-ups’ titles. It can’t always tell a pet’s name from a person’s name, so check the list now and then.
  • See them in the parent area: tap the child → What Mo remembers. Tap Forget to erase one. They’re also deleted with the child’s data.
  • Mo uses them only to bring one up in a later chat (for example, “How is Canela today?”). They go to OpenAI only as part of setting up a Mo chat.

Family voice recordings (optional)

  • A parent can invite a family member by sharing a private link. We store the name the parent types (for example “Grann Mari”) and which lines they record.
  • On the link’s web page, the family member records lines. Those recordings, and up to one voice message a week from the child back to them, are kept in private storage and played only in your child’s app and on that link.
  • Anyone with the link can use it, so share it only with family. Removing a link stops it right away and deletes its recordings and messages.
  • The family page doesn’t use cookies or ask the family member for an email, phone, or account.

“Join the test” sign-ups (familyspeak.app)

  • A parent can sign up for the test with the form on familyspeak.app. We store the parent’s email address, the kids’ ages (never names), the family language, whether you have an iPhone, the website language you used, and when you signed up.
  • We use it only to choose test families, to email you the TestFlight invite (in the language you used), and to write to you about the test. We never sell or share it.
  • Sign-ups aren’t linked to an app account and no IP addresses are stored with them. We keep them until the test ends, then delete them, or sooner if you ask.

Language requests (optional)

  • Parents can tell us their family’s language isn’t in FamilySpeak yet, in the app (“My language isn’t here”) or with the form on familyspeak.app.
  • We store the language and, only if you give it, an email address so we can tell you when that language is ready. We also note whether it came from the app or the website and which language our screens were in.
  • From the app, the request is linked to your account and deleted when you delete your account. Website requests aren’t linked to anyone. No IP addresses are stored with them.
  • We use requests only to decide which languages to build next and to send that one “it’s ready” email. We count them by language; we never sell or share them.

What we don’t collect

  • No last names, photos, video, location, contacts, or phone numbers for kids.
  • No advertising IDs, ads, analytics or tracking tools, and no push-notification service. Reminders are set up on your phone itself.
  • No payment details during the test.

4. How we use it

  • To run lessons, check answers, and adapt lessons to each child’s level.
  • To show the parent area and weekly report, and send the weekly email you can turn off.
  • To choose test families and send test invites.
  • To make speech checking and lessons better during the test.
  • We never use children’s data for ads, and never contact children directly.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your family’s data while your account is open, so progress isn’t lost if you take a break or a trial ends. Delete it anytime as described below. When the test ends, we will tell test families what happens to their accounts, and we will delete test data on request. Test sign-ups from the website are deleted when the test ends.

6. How to delete data

  • One child: Parent area → tap the child → Delete [name]’s data. This deletes the profile, progress, answers and transcripts, saved recordings, family voice recordings and messages, AI-voiced lines with their name, live chat minutes, and what Mo remembers.
  • Whole account: Parent area → Account → Delete my account. This deletes all of the above for every child, plus your login, settings, permission record and language requests.
  • A test sign-up or a language request from the website: email us from the address you gave and we’ll delete it.
  • Or email hello@familyspeak.app from your account email and we’ll do it for you.

Deletion is immediate in the app. Copies in our providers’ backups are removed as those backups expire. Emails already sent can’t be unsent.

7. Keeping it safe

  • Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Database rules let each parent reach only their own family’s data; recordings are in private storage. Only our admin can see test sign-ups.
  • Secret keys for our providers stay on our server, never in the app.
  • The parent area sits behind a grown-ups-only question, so kids can’t change settings.

8. Changes

If we change what we collect about children, we’ll update this page and ask parents for permission again in the app before the change applies.
