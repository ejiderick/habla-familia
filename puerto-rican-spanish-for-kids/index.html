Questions

Does FamilySpeak use Puerto Rican Spanish?

Lessons are written for every Spanish-speaking family. Set “Where is your family from?” to Caribbean, and the answers your kid gives with your family’s everyday words (like guagua or zafacón) count as right, along with words from every other Spanish-speaking country. The phone voices are the same for every family for now.

My child understands Spanish but won’t speak it. Can FamilySpeak help?

Yes. That’s exactly who it’s for: kids 6 to 12 who understand Spanish but answer in English. Every question is answered out loud, answers start short, and Mo never says “wrong”. Kids who answer in English hear the Spanish way to say it and try it back.

Do we need to speak the language at home every day?

No. The app does the practice. Family is a bonus: anyone in the family can record lesson lines from a link, with no app needed, and your kid hears their real voice.

How much does it cost?

$9.99 a month or $79.99 a year for one family plan, up to 4 kids, with a 7-day free trial. You can cancel anytime. It’s free while we’re in beta.

Is it on Android?

iPhone now. Android is coming.
