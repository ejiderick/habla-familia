1. Who can use FamilySpeak

  • Accounts are for parents and legal guardians who are 18 or older. You must give permission before your child uses the app.
  • Children 6 to 12 use FamilySpeak through their parent’s account, with the parent’s permission and supervision.
  • Family members you invite by link (for example, a grandparent) may use the family voice page for your child only.

2. FamilySpeak Plus subscriptions

  • FamilySpeak Plus is one family subscription for up to 4 kids, bought in the app through Apple: $9.99 a month or $79.99 a year (US prices; the App Store shows the price in your country, which may include tax).
  • New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. If you don’t cancel before the trial ends, the subscription starts and you’re charged.
  • The subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your Apple account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Manage or cancel anytime in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If you cancel, you keep FamilySpeak Plus until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app or your FamilySpeak account doesn’t cancel the subscription.
  • Apple handles payment and refunds. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. We never see your payment details.
  • Family Sharing: FamilySpeak Plus can be shared with the members of your Apple Family Sharing group.
  • Offer codes: we may give out offer codes, for example for free months. Redeem them in the parent area (Redeem a code) or in the App Store. The app shows when the offer ends; after that, the subscription renews at the regular price unless you cancel, as above.

3. Your account

  • You can create your account with an email address and password, or by signing in with Apple or Google (from app version 1.1.0).
  • Keep your password and your Apple or Google account private, and don’t let your child into the parent area.
  • Give accurate information, and enter only a first name or nickname for each child.
  • One family plan covers up to 4 kid profiles.
  • You can delete a child’s data or your whole account anytime in the parent area. See the Privacy Policy for what that deletes.

4. Family voice links and shared moments

  • Anyone with a family link can record lines and hear your child’s messages on it, so share it only with family members you trust.
  • You’re responsible for who you share links with. You can remove a link anytime, which stops it and deletes its recordings.
  • Family members should record only the lines shown, and nothing hurtful or private.
  • Shared moments: you can share one of your child’s voice messages, or their certificate, with a private link. Anyone with the link can open it for 30 days, unless you stop it sooner on your child’s page in the parent area. Share it only with people you trust.

5. Using FamilySpeak the right way

Please don’t misuse FamilySpeak. For example, don’t try to get into other families’ data, interfere with the service, copy or resell the lessons, or use Talk to Mo or the AI grandparent for anything other than a kid-friendly conversation.

6. Lessons, speech checking and AI

  • FamilySpeak helps kids practice speaking. It isn’t a school, a tutor, or a speech therapy service, and it can’t guarantee fluency.
  • Talk to Mo and the grandparent chats are live voice chats with an AI character. Each child can use them for up to 5 minutes a day in all, and each family for up to 150 minutes a month. You can turn them off in the parent area, under Settings → Live voice chats.
  • Mo’s voice is made with AI, in every language.
  • Speech checking, Talk to Mo, the AI grandparent and Mo’s voice use automated tools that can make mistakes, especially with young voices. Kids can always try again.
  • The Spanish (Habla) and Haitian Creole (Pale) lessons were reviewed by native speakers. We keep improving them, so some words may change.
