How it works

  1. Set up your family. Pick your family’s language and where your family is from, and add up to 4 kids.
  2. Your kid answers out loud. Short daily lessons about real home life: food, family, school, feelings. Your kid hears a question and answers by speaking. Mo, their buddy, listens and gently helps. Never a red X, never “wrong”.
  3. They take it home. Each day ends with a chat with Mo and a tiny mission to say something to someone in the family. The weekly report shows you how much they spoke, the phrases they can now answer, and the week’s family missions.

Questions

My kid understands but won’t speak. Is this for them?

Yes. That’s exactly who it’s for: kids who understand the family’s language but answer in English.

Which Spanish?

Every Spanish. We accept words from every Spanish-speaking family, wherever your family is from.

Is it safe?

Yes. No ads. Parent controls, time limits and kid-safe chats. Only a parent can create the account, and we never ask for a child’s last name, photo or location. Privacy Policy

Do we need to speak the language at home every day?

No. The app does the practice. Family is a bonus.

iPhone only?

iPhone now. Android is coming.

Can I cancel?

Anytime.
