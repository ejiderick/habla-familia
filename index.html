How it works

  1. Set up in a minute. Pick your family’s language and add up to 4 kids. Each kid gets their own front door on Mo’s street.
  2. They play by talking. Your kid holds the button and answers out loud. Mo listens and gently helps. Never a red X, never “wrong”.
  3. They take it home. A tiny mission to say something to someone in the family, and a weekly email with how much they spoke and a phrase to try at dinner.

Mo’s street

Home is a little street. Mo’s house has today’s short lesson, and the other places hold games, stories and songs. Every place has its name in Spanish or Haitian Creole, and its English is one tap away.

26 games they play by talking

Sing with Mo, catch Mo’s mistakes, race with your voice, fly a balloon by speaking, guess riddles, play I spy and hide-and-seek. Each game takes a few minutes, and the only way to play is to speak.

A family that answers back

In the lessons, your kid talks with the family: Mamá, Papá, a tía (in Haitian Creole, Manman, Papa, Grann…). They answer what your kid actually says, so it feels like a real conversation, not a quiz. Your kid can also practice a phone call with family. Family can record lines from their own phone, no app needed, and your child hears their voice in the lessons.

A daily call with Mo

A short voice call: Mo asks about what your kid is learning, your kid answers out loud, and Mo helps when they get stuck.

  • Mo only says lines we wrote and checked. Never a chatbot.
  • A daily time limit
  • A parent on/off switch

A shop where they ask out loud, and a mailbox

Kids earn coins as they play and spend them at Mo’s shop, by asking for what they want, out loud, in the family’s language. The mailbox has something new every day, and a Friday gift after a week of playing.

A card game for the dinner table: 18 questions your child answers out loud. Print at home, no email needed.

Questions

My kid understands but won’t speak. Is this for them?

Yes. That’s exactly who it’s for: kids who understand the family’s language but answer in English.

Does it feel like homework?

No. There’s nothing to grade and no red X. Kids play by talking, a few minutes a day.

Which Spanish?

Every Spanish. We accept words from every Spanish-speaking family, wherever your family is from.

Is it safe?

Yes. No ads. Parent controls, time limits and kid-safe chats. Only a parent can create the account, and we never ask for a child’s last name, photo or location. Privacy Policy

Do we need to speak the language at home every day?

No. The app does the practice. Family is a bonus.

iPhone only?

iPhone now. Android is coming.

Can I cancel?

Anytime.
