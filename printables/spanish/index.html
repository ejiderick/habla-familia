Free printable Spanish game for kids

Dinner Table Questions: 18 cards that get kids 6 to 12 answering in Spanish, out loud. Each card has a question in Spanish, the English for you, and answer ideas.

How to play

  1. Print it double-sided on cardstock and cut along the dotted lines.
  2. Draw a card and ask the question in Spanish.
  3. Your child answers out loud, in Spanish. Any answer counts. Stuck? Read an answer idea and let them say it.

The questions and answer ideas come from FamilySpeak’s lessons, checked by native speakers. Free for home and classroom use.
